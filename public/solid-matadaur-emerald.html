Color through the grain.

Choose your center color below, then use Top burst in the showroom. A burst adds a soft transition through color at the perimeter; No burst carries your chosen stain across the carved top.

Finish references: PRS Custom 24 · Gibson Honey Burst · Gibson Iced Tea / Dark Cherry. These are color references; the previews retain the original Fostinis shapes.

Maple figure, ash grain, walnut and mahogany use separate texture assets. A color selection never changes the body shape. Natural grain and physical stain results vary; these previews are not color-matched finish samples.

The Fostinis story · New York / New Jersey

An original shape.
A personal instrument.

Luthier Jimmy Fostinis and artist Joseph Califano brought their ideas together around one ambition: guitars with distinctive looks, an effortless feel, and a voice of their own.

That collaboration lives in three original designs. Start with the shape that speaks to you, then explore your own combination of woods, finishes, and appointments in the showroom.
